    Modes and keys

    Watch 1 2
    Overview and Ground cameras. Drag to turn, wheel or right-drag to zoom, middle-drag to tilt. Esc comes back here from any mode
    Select
    Click a vehicle to select it, and click open ground to clear
    Follow F
    The camera rides behind the selected vehicle. Drag and zoom to move it round the vehicle
    Drive E
    Your baggage tractor. WASD or arrows; S brakes then reverses; steering at a standstill turns slowly on the spot. 25 km/h cap (assumed). Vehicles you block wait, then reroute after 10 s if the roads allow; the time they lose is the delay counter. Late vehicles push the turn's off-block back
    Walk X
    From the centre of the view. WASD walk, Q E turn, Shift runs, Space jumps, drag to look. You cannot walk through vehicles or the aircraft; the traffic keeps to its schedule. Click a vehicle to ride along, click again to step off
    Mark
    Every detector event lands in the list on the right: give it a verdict (valid, false alarm, unsure). N flags something the detector missed. Click an event to look at it. Export writes the eval set as JSON
    Time
    Space plays or pauses, [ ] change speed, drag the timeline to jump (a jump replays the schedule without your driving). The demo plays two turnarounds with a gap, then loops
    Views
    L legend, K events, C hides every panel, H this panel
